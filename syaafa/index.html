Data Analytics: Turning Data Into Decisions

Welcome to my Data Analytics website, part of the Digital Workplace Readiness Portal.

Data analytics helps transform raw information into meaningful insights. In the modern workplace, organisations use these insights to understand performance, identify patterns and support better decision-making.

What is Data Analytics?

Data analytics is the process of collecting, organising, examining and interpreting data to discover useful information and identify patterns or trends.

Organisations generate large amounts of data through customers, employees, sales, websites and digital systems. Data analytics helps convert this information into insights that can support organisational goals.

Why is Data Analytics Important?

Data analytics has become an important part of the digital workplace because organisations need accurate information when making decisions.

  • Better decision-making: Organisations can make decisions based on evidence rather than assumptions.
  • Identify trends: Data can reveal patterns and changes that may not be immediately obvious.
  • Improve performance: Organisations can monitor their performance and identify areas that require improvement.
  • Understand customers: Customer data can help organisations understand preferences and behaviour.
